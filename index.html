Purrfect Order

A cozy first-person game about putting every cat to bed.

Each fur colour has its own kind of bed. Find them, scoop them up, carry them over, and watch them curl up and purr.

Seek & scoop

Where did they all go?

Sixty to a hundred and eighty cats are loose in every level. Some nap in plain sight. Others are tucked into drawers, lockers, overhead bins and the claw machine.

Listen closely. A hidden cat gives itself away with a muffled meow, and leaps out the moment you open its hiding spot. Pick it up and it either wriggles or dozes off in your arms, depending on its mood.

The one rule

Every coat has its bed

Sorting goes by fur colour, never by collar or hat. Put a cat in the right spot and it curls up and sleeps through. Put it in the wrong one and it protests, hops out and wanders off to think about what you did.

Eleven coats, eleven kinds of bed. The white cats even get a pet carrier whose shutter closes once the right cat is asleep inside.

Built for the feeling

No timer. No fail state.

The clock only counts up, so you can chase a best time or ignore it entirely. Nothing chases you and nothing runs out.

Stop and pet a cat whenever you like. Scratch its head and it purrs, stroke its back and it arches, and if you go for the tail you will hear about it.

Tools of the trade

A pocketful of mischief

Cats are fast and you only have two hands. Lure them, carry them in bulk, or roll them across the floor wrapped in bubble wrap. When a cat is up on the gallery, float up after it.

Play your way

Comfy settings

Cozy means comfortable, so the controls and settings bend to you. Play with mouse and keyboard or a controller, rebind every button, and turn the helpers on or off.

There is nothing to fail and nothing to rush, so you can put the game down whenever you like and pick it up again later.

Controller supportXbox and PlayStation pads with aim assist and vibration. The game pauses if the controller disconnects.
Rebind everythingKeyboard and controller layouts are fully remappable.
Field of viewAdjustable FOV, mouse sensitivity and crosshair.
Bed labelsOptional signs tell you which coat belongs in which bed.
Hint buttonPress H for an arrow to the next loose cat or the right bed.
Separate volumesMusic, sound effects, and weather & ambience each have their own slider.
Ultrawide readyBuilt and tested on a 21:9 screen, with upscaling and quality presets for smaller PCs.
Time of day & weatherPick sunshine, clouds or snow, and morning or night.
